# Matterbase for Home Assistant

Keep a private inventory of Matter pairing codes. Scan QR codes or printed codes,
recognise label names, and preserve notes, colours, icons, and original QR payloads.

The add-on uses the same app as standalone Docker. Its inventory lives in its own
private storage and is never imported into Home Assistant automatically.

Open **Documentation** for installation, migration, backups, and optional pairing help.
